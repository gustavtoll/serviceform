# Changelog

All notable changes to the Mira E-commerce Prospecting Playbook are documented here.

## 2026-08-28

### Changed

- Reskinned the playbook with a Serviceform-inspired visual system based on the live homepage: warm cream surfaces, espresso typography and navigation, warm orange emphasis, muted sage and sky accents, and pastel content cards.
- Reworked section surfaces, controls, borders, radii, spacing, and shadows to create a softer, more spacious product-marketing presentation without copying Serviceform content or assets.
- Replaced the editorial serif treatment with a clean geometric sans display while retaining all playbook copy and semantic structure.
- Preserved the skip link, keyboard focus visibility, landmarks, heading hierarchy, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior.
