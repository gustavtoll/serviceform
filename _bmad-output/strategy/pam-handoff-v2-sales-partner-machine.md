# PAM Handoff v2 — Sales Partner Machine

## Status

Wave 2 enablement is implemented on `feature/sales-partner-wave-two` as two independent Vercel-ready static applications. This handoff authorizes review and use of the demonstrations; it does not activate the Sales Partner program, GoHighLevel, calendars, attribution, payouts, public claims, or competition mechanics.

## Implemented streams

### W2-A — Sales Partner resource hub

`apps/sales-partner-resource-hub` provides:

- a first-14-day start path;
- Independent, Creator, and Agency / Company tracks;
- e-commerce and five-vertical fit guidance;
- opening, introduction, and bridge scripts;
- an opportunity checklist;
- source and referral discipline;
- a guarded Sub-Sales Partner explanation;
- a 90-day pilot rhythm;
- objections and FAQs; and
- decision and terms guardrails.

It is static, private-oriented, `noindex,nofollow`, and collects no data.

### W2-B — E-commerce Sales Partner deck

`apps/ecommerce-sales-partner-deck` provides an accessible slide/section story covering:

- unanswered product and support questions;
- product fit, assortment complexity, cart uncertainty, and repetitive support;
- guided shopper conversations across discovery, comparison, choice, completion, and learning;
- general examples for auto parts, supplements/nutrition, florists, fishing/specialty, and furniture;
- Sales Partner positioning and first-call discovery;
- a useful source-rich handoff; and
- claims and terms safety.

It supports keyboard navigation and responsive/reduced-motion behavior. It is `noindex,nofollow`.

## Source and claims control

Grounding used only the permanent PDFs under `Assets/` and the Wave 0 authority. Unapproved numerical results, named customer proof, quotes, logos, and testimonials were deliberately excluded. Vertical cases are labeled as general examples. Nutrition content is limited to product-information guidance and explicitly excludes medical, diagnostic, and treatment claims.

## Program inputs, not promises

- 40% lifetime commission.
- 90-day first-click cookie/crediting.
- Dream Car lease continuity at 100 active deals.
- 10% Sub-Sales Partner kickback.

Signed terms must still define calculation bases, lifetime, eligibility, attribution exceptions, payment timing, refunds, taxes, active deal, verification, competition rules, and termination.

## Untouched gates

W3 calculator, proof/testimonial factory, and product deck remain gated for a separate authorization. GoHighLevel workflows, calendars, CRM mutation, tracking, email, payouts, public claims, customer proof, and Dream Car mechanics remain inactive.

## Review contract

Reviewers should verify the applications against the brand kit, claims register, program charter, and the deployment evidence in `DEPLOYMENT.md`. Merge review may follow the pushed branch; the implementing agent must not merge to `main`.
