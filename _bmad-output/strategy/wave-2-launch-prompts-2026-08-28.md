# Wave 2 launch prompts — 28 August 2026

These prompts are controlled enablement starters, not automated sends or approved public campaigns. Replace bracketed context with verified observations and preserve the source URL.

## Independent Sales Partner

### First conversation

> I noticed shoppers have to make several choices around [fit/product/delivery]. How do you currently help someone who is unsure what matches their need?

### Introduction

> I would like to introduce you to the Serviceform team. They support guided website conversations across product discovery and common support questions. I thought the fit was worth exploring because [verified store signal]. Would a short journey-mapping conversation be useful?

## Creator Sales Partner

### Audience prompt

> E-commerce teams work hard to bring the right shopper to a store. What happens when that shopper cannot quickly answer a product, fit, or delivery question? I am exploring how guided on-site conversations can make that journey clearer. No outcome promise—just a practical look at one use case.

### Follow-up

> Which question creates the most hesitation for your shoppers today, and where in the journey does it appear?

## Agency / Company Sales Partner

### Portfolio opener

> Across the clients you support, where do product complexity or repetitive support questions create the most visible friction? Serviceform can explore guided shopper conversations alongside the acquisition and commerce work you already deliver.

### Co-selling handoff

> Client: [store]. Verified signal: [observation + URL]. Current response: [confirmed process]. Priority journey: [one use case]. Contact and permission context: [details]. Proposed next step: [agreed review].

## First-call discovery

1. Who is the shopper and what are they trying to decide?
2. Where do product, compatibility, delivery, return, or order questions appear?
3. How are those questions handled today, and by whom?
4. Which approved catalog data, guides, FAQs, and policies could support answers?
5. Which single journey is useful enough to explore first?
6. Who should join a scoped follow-up?

## Vertical prompts — general examples only

- **Auto parts / aftermarket:** Where do shoppers hesitate when confirming fit, compatibility, or related components?
- **Supplements / nutrition:** Which approved product attributes help a shopper compare format, flavor, dietary preference, routine, or subscription? Do not diagnose, treat, or promise health outcomes.
- **Online florists:** Which occasion, recipient, delivery, and add-on questions intensify during seasonal peaks?
- **Fishing / specialty:** What setup, use-case, environment, or compatibility knowledge currently depends on a staff expert?
- **Furniture:** Which style, dimensions, delivery, product, and order questions interrupt the journey? The fuller Furniture ICP remains pending.
