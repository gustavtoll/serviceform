02 / Find the fit

Look for complexity a conversation can clarify.

Auto parts

Fitment, VIN/YMM, compatibility, large catalogs, related parts.

Supplements / nutrition

Goal, format, flavor, routine, comparison, subscription. Use approved product information; never diagnose or treat.

Online florists

Occasion, recipient, budget, delivery timing, add-ons, seasonal demand.

Fishing / specialty

Use case, skill level, environment, setup, compatible accessories.

Furniture

Style, dimensions, delivery, product questions, order status. ICP definition is still being developed.

Conversation opener

“I noticed shoppers have to make a few complex choices on your site. How do you currently help someone who is unsure what fits their need?”

Trusted introduction

“I’d like to introduce you to the Serviceform team. They work on guided website conversations across product discovery and support. I thought the fit was worth exploring because of [specific store signal].”

Creator / agency bridge

“You already invest in getting the right shopper to the store. What happens when that person has a product or delivery question before buying?”

04 / Source discipline

Attribution starts before the link exists.

Capture

Where did it begin?

Record the page URL, observed signal, date, contact, Sales Partner, campaign context, and any parent Sub-Sales Partner relationship.

Confirm

Is the account known?

Flag existing conversations and duplicates for review. Never promise credit before the record is checked.

Preserve

What happened next?

Keep the first-touch evidence and every handoff state. The working input is a 90-day first-click cookie/crediting rule, subject to approved terms.

Referral network

Sales Partner → Sub-Sales Partner → opportunity

A Sub-Sales Partner follows the same approval and source rules. The 10% Sub-Sales Partner kickback is a program input; its calculation base, eligibility, and payment mechanics remain unresolved and inactive.

06 / Objections & FAQ

Stay useful when the answer is “maybe.”

“We already have chat.”

Ask what the current experience helps shoppers do: find a product, resolve fit questions, compare choices, or locate order information. Discovery comes before differentiation.

“Can you guarantee a result?”

No. Do not promise outcomes. Explore the current buying journey, define a useful use case, and let Serviceform scope the next step.

“How does commission work?”

40% lifetime commission is a program input, not a payout promise. “Lifetime,” calculation base, timing, refunds, tax, eligibility, and termination require approved signed terms.

“What about the Dream Car?”

The input is lease continuity at 100 active deals. Active-deal definition, markets, verification, tax, lease holder, grace, churn, and off-ramp remain gated. Do not promote it publicly.

“Can we start collecting leads here?”

No. This hub is static enablement only. It has no login, CRM connection, form collection, calendar activation, or attribution engine.

07 / Decision & terms guardrails

Know what is live—and what is not.

This is a Wave 2 enablement demonstration. It does not activate the Sales Partner program or create legal, payout, attribution, contest, or customer commitments.

  • Inputs: 40% lifetime commission; 90-day first-click; Dream Car lease continuity at 100 active deals; 10% Sub-Sales Partner kickback.
  • Still gated: signed terms, definitions, payout operations, public claims, customer proof, GoHighLevel, calendars, workflows, and competition mechanics.
  • Safe language: describe product capabilities and general scenarios; do not use unapproved customer names, testimonials, or numerical results.
