09 · Guardrails

Claims and terms safety

A presentation can explain general capabilities. It cannot create evidence or authorization.

  • No customer name, logo, quote, screenshot, or result without documented rights and approval.
  • No numerical outcome, guaranteed performance, medical claim, or unsupported comparison.
  • No partner payout, attribution, or competition statement without approved terms.
  • Escalate uncertainty; do not improvise a public claim.
