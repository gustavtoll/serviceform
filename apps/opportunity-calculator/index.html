Method

Formula, without a black box

Chats = traffic × interaction rate. Leads = chats × lead rate. Potential created = leads × average revenue per sale. Monthly opportunity = potential created × closing rate.

All values are planning assumptions. Validate them for the specific store and context before using the model in a conversation.
