05 / General vertical scenarios

Fitment, VIN/YMM, compatibility, related parts, installation context.

Goal, format, flavor, dietary preference, product comparison, subscription.

Guardrail: Approved product information only—no diagnosis, treatment, or medical outcome claims.

Ask: Which delivery and choice questions peak seasonally?

Use case, experience, environment, season, setup, compatible accessories.

Ask: What specialist knowledge lives mainly with staff?

06 / Sales Partner position

Open the right conversation.
Don’t force the close.

Position Serviceform around a visible shopper journey—not as a generic “AI chatbot” and not with an unsupported performance promise.

Observe“I noticed choosing the right [product] requires several decisions.”

Explore“How do shoppers get help when they are unsure?”

Connect“Serviceform supports guided website conversations across discovery and common support.”

Invite“Would it be useful to map one journey with the team?”

09 / Claims & terms safety

Capabilities are discussable.
Unapproved proof is not.

Safe to explore

  • Product discovery and category guidance
  • Product fit and comparison questions
  • Smart prompts across buying stages
  • Order, delivery, return, and common support flows
  • General vertical scenarios clearly labeled as examples

Approval required

  • Numerical sales, conversion, AOV, cart, intent, or support results
  • Customer names, logos, quotes, cases, or testimonials
  • Performance guarantees or universal outcomes
  • Medical, diagnostic, or treatment language
  • Legal, payout, attribution, or Dream Car promises

Program inputs—not signed terms: 40% lifetime commission; 90-day first-click; Dream Car lease continuity at 100 active deals; 10% Sub-Sales Partner kickback.
