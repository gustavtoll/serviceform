11 / Risks

Make the failure modes visible early.

Policy ambiguitySigned terms before activation; single source of truth.

Attribution disputesFirst-click record plus auditable exception states.

Unfunded liabilityChurn-adjusted reporting and finance approval.

Low-quality volumeApproval, qualification, and tier health—not raw leads.

Network abuseExplicit parent approval, identity, and duplicate controls.

OverclaimingClaims register; no unapproved metrics or customer proof.

Privacy/securityServer-side credentials, minimum data, consent and retention.

Operational sprawlWave gates, named owners, and rollback-ready rollout.
