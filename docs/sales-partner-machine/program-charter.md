# Sales Partner Program Charter — Wave 0

## Purpose
Create a reliable Sales Partner machine that attracts qualified e-commerce-connected partners, helps them create qualified opportunities, credits revenue correctly, and turns results into advocacy.

## Approved program decisions

| Policy | Decision |
|---|---|
| Program vocabulary | Sales Partner throughout new material |
| Revenue share | **40% lifetime commission**, subject to signed program terms |
| Attribution | **90-day first-click cookie and crediting rule** |
| Dream Car Competition | A vehicle **lease** remains active only while the qualifying Sales Partner maintains **100 active deals** |
| Network growth | A Sales Partner may recruit Sub-Sales Partners and earns a **10% kickback** from that relationship |
| Calendar | Existing calendar is already set up; calendar configuration is deferred until a later wave needs it |
| CRM location | GoHighLevel location will be supplied by Gustav through text before CRM implementation begins |

## Required definitions before public launch

- **Active deal:** a referred customer with a signed, billed, non-churned Serviceform contract; exact billing-grace and refund treatment must be confirmed by leadership.
- **Lifetime:** commission duration must be reflected precisely in the executed partner agreement and payout policy.
- **10% kickback:** specify whether calculated from the Sub-Sales Partner’s commission, referred revenue, or another base. Default implementation must remain blocked until confirmed.
- **Competition:** rules must define eligible markets, verification cadence, lease holder, tax/benefit treatment, replacement vehicle policy, and what happens after a customer pauses or churns.

## Partner tracks

| Track | Primary motion | Program support |
|---|---|---|
| Independent Sales Partner | trusted introductions and outbound | personal links, scripts, calendar handoff, daily activity plan |
| Creator Sales Partner | audience campaigns and content | campaign kits, tracked landing pages, webinar and content support |
| Agency / Company Sales Partner | client portfolio and co-selling | company attribution, multi-user reporting, co-marketing, account-manager support |
| Sub-Sales Partner | network-sourced introductions | parent relationship, approval path, transparent attribution |

## Success measures
Applications; approval rate; first qualified opportunity within 14 days; meeting-to-contract rate; active referred MRR; churn-adjusted commission liability; active Sales Partners; active Sub-Sales Partners; and attributable advocacy.
