# Serviceform Sales Partner Machine

This directory is the build authority for evolving Serviceform from calendar-only referral tracking into a Sales Partner acquisition, enablement, attribution, and advocacy system.

## Terminology
Use **Sales Partner**, **Sub-Sales Partner**, **Independent Sales Partner**, **Creator Sales Partner**, and **Agency / Company Sales Partner** in all new copy, UI, CRM labels, and technical documentation. Legacy source-file names are retained only as source references.

## Wave sequence

1. **Wave 0 — Foundation:** charter, policy, attribution, GoHighLevel contract, claims control.
2. **Wave 1 — Acquisition and leadership alignment:** application page and internal leadership presentation.
3. **Wave 2 — Enablement:** resource hub, outbound material, e-commerce Sales Partner deck.
4. **Wave 3 — Selling tools and proof:** calculator, testimonial factory, interactive e-commerce product presentation.
5. **Wave 4 — Pilot and advocacy:** controlled rollout, tier progression, Sub-Sales Partner growth, competition launch.

No CRM workflow, production deployment, public claim, payout, or contest is activated by these planning documents.

## Wave 3 delivery

Wave 3 comprises three independent noindex artifacts: a transparent illustrative opportunity calculator, a zero-approved-assets internal proof readiness factory, and a keyboard-accessible e-commerce product presentation. The calculator alone is a separately authorized, limited production planning tool; W3-B and W3-C remain undeployed and gated. See the [Wave 3 implementation plan](../plans/2026-08-29-sales-partner-machine-wave-three.md), [Wave 0–3 delivery register](wave-three-delivery-register.md), and [Vercel release guardrails](vercel-release-guardrails.md). Technical deployment evidence does not authorize publication, customer proof, CRM activity, payouts, terms, or competition mechanics.
