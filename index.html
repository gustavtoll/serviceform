04

Qualification rubric

Research the friction, not just the firmographics.

A strong prospect gives visible evidence that product choice or repetitive support is costly.

  1. 01

    Choice complexity

    Variants, compatibility rules, technical specifications, or occasion-driven selection.

    High value
  2. 02

    Assisted-selling evidence

    Quizzes, buying guides, chat, fitment tools, comparison content, or “contact an expert.”

    Intent
  3. 03

    Support repetition

    Prominent FAQs, delivery questions, returns friction, setup help, and product-care content.

    Volume
  4. 04

    Commercial upside

    Healthy order values, bundles, accessories, replenishment, or subscription potential.

    Impact
  5. 05

    Execution readiness

    A credible e-commerce experience, meaningful traffic, and a reachable commercial owner.

    Feasibility
05

Research to outreach

Build the case before writing the message.

Every outreach angle should trace back to something observable on the merchant’s storefront.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Find category leaders and specialist retailers.

  2. 02

    Validate

    Capture source URLs for every prospect claim.

  3. 03

    Score

    Rank friction, upside, and readiness.

  4. 04

    Personalize

    Name one shopper decision Mira can improve.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Test the angle, learn, and refine the segment.
